## What is software engineering? Explain what it includes.

Software engineering is a systematic way of analyzing, designing, developing and maintaining software systems.

## How is engineering process divided into different phases? What activities are involved in each phase?

It is divided into three phases:

- Definition phase: this included defining the requirements for the product to be developed, setting key milestones and acceptance criteria and prioritizing tasks.

- Development phase: consists of designing and developing functionalities and features according to prioritized goals. This is followed by the testing of the developed software.

- Maintenance phase: this phase is for making future modifications, updates and optimizing the system after it is already in use.

## Why do we need software development process models?

Provides a clean roadmap to keep teams aligned and organize. Catches bugs faster when they are significantly cheaper to fix. Establishes predictable schedules- timelines- and major provider to milestone. Enforces standardized testing and validation procedures throughout development.

## How Spiral model differ from Waterfall model?

Waterfall strictly linear and sequential. Spiral iterative and uses continuous loops.

## What are the advantages of Modified Waterfall model over Pure Waterfall model?

The advantages are as follows:
- Modified waterfall s more flexible than the pure waterfall
- In modified waterfall, developers can choose first to work on easy part of the project without waiting for difficult sections
- Documentation can be reduced if there is personal continuity between the phases

## What are the advantages of iterative development?

The advantages of the iterative development are as follows:
- Defects can be detected early
- Project can be upgraded according to the customer needs
- High-level risks can be identified and addressed during individual iterations.

## What are the advantages of RUP over other development process models?

The advantages of **RUP** over other development process models are:
- **RUP** splits project into small, manageable iterations
- Can accommodate the changes in the middle of project according to the requirement
- It helps reduce projects risk profile and allow greater customer feedback and help developers stay focused
